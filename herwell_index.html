🌸 PCOS / PCOD basics

PCOS is a hormonal condition that can affect periods and other aspects of health. Experiences vary. Lifestyle support can be part of care, but it does not replace assessment or treatment when needed.

Helpful next step: Ask a clinician which symptoms to track and what care options are appropriate.

WHO: PCOS fact sheet ↗

🥗 Balanced eating

A balanced pattern can include vegetables and fruit, whole grains, protein sources and fats. Needs and access differ; there is no one perfect diet for everyone with PCOS.

Try: Build meals around foods you enjoy and can access, rather than restrictive rules.

NHS: PCOS overview ↗

🚶 Movement and wellbeing

Walking, cycling, dancing, yoga, stretching or strength activities may be options depending on ability and preference. Start gradually and choose movement that feels manageable.

WHO: Physical activity ↗

🩺 Period concerns and getting care

Period changes can have many causes. Note dates, pain, bleeding changes and other symptoms to discuss with a healthcare professional, especially if changes persist.

Seek urgent care for severe or sudden pain, fainting, very heavy bleeding or symptoms that feel like an emergency.
